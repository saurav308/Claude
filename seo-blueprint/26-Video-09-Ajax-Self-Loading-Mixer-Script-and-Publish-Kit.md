## 0. The video in one breath

In most of India the self-loading concrete mixer is simply "Ajax machine", the way an excavator is a Poclain. It scoops its own sand and aggregate, weighs the cement, mixes on the move and pours where the slab is: a loader, a weigh-batcher, a mixer and a transit mixer in one 4WD machine. On DesiMachines' listings the range is ₹33–46 lakh ex-factory by drum size, from the 2 m³ Argo 2000 to the 4.5–4.6 m³ class from Ajax, Schwing Stetter, Venus, Apollo and Conmat, across eleven brands and 38 models. The video answers the two questions nobody puts in the same sentence: *for which site does this machine make sense* (the RMC-distance test), and *which capacity* (the day's concrete divided by the batches a site really gets). It closes with the per-cubic-metre hisaab and a hire-business ledger, and the rule: *RMC ke saamne SLCM haar jaati hai, RMC ke peeche jeet-ti hai.*
