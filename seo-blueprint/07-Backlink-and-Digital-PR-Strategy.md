# 07 — Backlink & Digital PR Strategy

> Referring-domain data [FACT] from Ahrefs (25 Jun 2026). DesiMachines has **573 live referring domains, DR 41** — a strong base. But the profile has a **material toxicity problem** that must be addressed before aggressive new link-building.

## 1. ⚠️ CRITICAL FINDING — toxic / spammy link cluster

The top-DR referring domains include a cluster that is unmistakably **purchased/spam SEO links**, not editorial. Examples pulled live:

| Domain | DR | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| `itxoft.com`, `itxoft-affordable-seo-solutions.site`, `itxoft-seo-for-higher-rankings.site` | 75–83 | SEO link-farm network |
| `fiverr-seo-for-small-businesses.site`, `fiverr-cost-effective-seo.site` | 71 | Fiverr-spun spam sites |
| `buybacklinks.agency` | 72 | Literally a paid-link vendor |
| `optimize-instant.website`, `seoexpress.org`, `rankyour.website`, `rankva.com` | 71–75 | Auto-generated SEO spam |
| `siteprice.org`, `bizidex.com`, `apsense.com`, `zupyak.com`, `sooperarticles.com` | 66–73 | Low-value directory/article spam |

**Why this matters for a *lead* business:**
- **Risk:** This footprint is consistent with a past low-quality link-buying campaign. It rarely earns a manual penalty on its own today, but it (a) adds algorithmic risk, (b) wastes "DR" that doesn't convert, and (c) signals low trust to the EEAT systems that increasingly gate AI-Overview citations.
- **It is not driving leads.** Most of these have `traffic_domain: 0` — no referral traffic, no business value.

### [REC] Action — disavow, don't panic
1. **Export the full referring-domain list** (Ahrefs) and tag every domain: `editorial / directory-ok / spam`.
2. **Build a `disavow.txt`** for the clear spam cluster (the `.site` SEO-spam domains, buybacklinks.agency, link-farm networks). Submit via GSC.
3. **Stop any ongoing paid-link activity** immediately — it's net-negative for a trust-dependent lead brand.
4. Re-audit monthly until clean (`01` cadence).

> This is the single most important *risk* item in the blueprint. It does not block growth, but left unmanaged it caps the trust ceiling that AI citation and Google's helpful-content systems reward.

## 3. Link & digital-PR acquisition plan (prioritized by authority × relevance × lead value)

### Tier 1 — Industry-relevant, lead-adjacent (do first)
| Target type | Examples / approach | Lead value | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Construction trade publications** | EquipmentIndia, Construction World, ConstroFacilitator, RealtyPlus, NBM&CW, Equipment Times — pitch data stories & expert commentary | High (audience = buyers) | Med |
| **Industry associations** | iCEMA (Indian Construction Equipment Mfrs Assoc), Builders' Assoc of India, CREDAI, NHAI/infra bodies — membership, resource listings, data partnerships | High | Med-High |
| **OEM & dealer co-marketing** | Onboarded OEMs/dealers link to their DesiMachines model/brand pages; "find us on DesiMachines" | High (also referral leads) | Low-Med |
| **Financier / insurer partners** | Already onboarded — earn links from their partner pages | High | Low |

### Tier 2 — Data-driven digital PR (the durable engine)
| Campaign | Hook | Who links |
|---|---|---|
| **DesiMachines Price Index** (quarterly) | "Construction equipment prices in India rose/fell X% in Q_" | Trade press, business news, OEMs |
| **State infrastructure equipment demand report** | Tie to budget/infra spend by state | Regional news, govt-adjacent |
| **"Most-searched machines in India" report** | First-party search/intent data | News, trade, AI engines |
| **Used vs new ownership-cost study** | TCO data no OEM publishes | Finance + trade press |

### Tier 3 — Foundational & scalable (steady drip)
- Quality business/industry directories (justdial, sulekha, IndiaMART seller profile, Google Business Profile, equipment-specific directories) — relevance + NAP consistency for local.
- Guest expert columns (founder's 6-yr dealership background = strong author bio → EEAT).
- Podcast / YouTube interviews in the infra/construction niche.
- HARO/Qwoted-style journalist sourcing for construction-economy stories.
- Broken-link building on trade resource pages (find dead OEM/spec links, offer DesiMachines pages).

### Tier 4 — Reverse-engineer competitors
- Export **tractorjunction** and **91infra** backlinks; filter to editorial/association/directory links; replicate the relevant ones. (Use Ahrefs `site-explorer-all-backlinks` on those domains — available on request.)

## 4. Anchor-text & velocity guidance [REC]
- Keep anchors **branded + natural** ("DesiMachines", URL, "construction equipment platform") for PR/editorial; reserve exact-match commercial anchors for internal links, not external (the spam cluster likely over-used exact-match — another reason to disavow).
- **Slow, steady velocity** of high-quality links beats bursts; the AI-citation/PR flywheel compounds.

## 5. 90-day link priorities
1. **Disavow the spam cluster** (Week 1–2) — risk reduction.
2. **OEM/dealer/financier partner links** (Week 2–6) — easy, relevant, lead-adjacent.
3. **One flagship data-PR campaign** — the **DesiMachines Price Index** (Month 2–3) — links + brand + AIO citations + leads.
4. **Trade-press relationships** seeded for ongoing coverage.
