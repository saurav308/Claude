## 3. How DesiMachines wins each tier (summary)

- **vs Marketplaces (indiamart/tradeindia):** Win on **depth + conversion**. Per-machine buyer guides, real prices, compare tool, and a faster quote path than their generic "Send Enquiry."
- **vs 91infra / machanx / machineryline:** Win on **authority + execution**. DesiMachines already has 5–10× the DR; simply out-build their ranking pages and ship the missing funnels (used/rental).
- **vs tractorjunction:** Win on **CE focus + neutrality + AI visibility**. They're tractor-first; DesiMachines is construction-equipment-native. Match their funnels (used/finance/dealer) while owning CE vernacular and AI-Overview citations they're not optimizing for.
- **vs OEMs:** Win on **neutral comparison + price transparency + used/finance**. The buyer's job-to-be-done ("which machine, what price, can I afford it, who sells it near me") is something **no single OEM will ever answer** — that's structurally DesiMachines' to own.
