## 0. The video in one breath

"Hydra ya Farana?" is the wrong question, and the video says so in the first minute: both names are borrowed brands for the same machine class, the pick-and-carry crane that lifts and walks with the load. On DesiMachines' listings the 12–15 tonne hydra tier is ₹16–22 lakh and the farana tier starts around ₹32 lakh for the same 15 tonnes, a ₹13–15 lakh jump that buys four-wheel drive, articulated steering, a longer boom and a bigger engine. The real question is: what do you lift every day, where does it have to reach, and how many days a month will someone pay ₹18,000–35,000 for it. The hisaab shows a 14-tonne hydra clearing its EMI from about four billed days a month, which is why the business is forgiving on the machine and unforgiving on the rate you assumed. The rule: *count the billed days in your district before you count the tonnes.* Close on *Suno Sabki, Chuno Apni* and the brand principle.
