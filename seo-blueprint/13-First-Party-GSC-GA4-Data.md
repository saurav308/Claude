**Two compounding levers, both quantified:**
1. **Move position 4–10 → 1–3.** There are **31,935 keywords and 2.8M monthly-ish impressions in positions 4–10** earning only 12,082 clicks. This is the `04` quick-win harvest, now backed by real impression volume. Even a 20% click-share improvement here = thousands of incremental high-intent visits/mo.
2. **Improve CTR at current positions.** A 0.43% CTR in positions 4–10 (and 0.73% even in 1–3) is *low* — driven by AI Overviews and SERP features eating clicks, and by titles/rich-results not optimized. **Adding Product/Offer + FAQ schema, price in the title, and review stars (`03` C1/C2, `09`) lifts CTR with no ranking change** — pure incremental clicks from impressions you already own.

> **Caveat:** Much of the position-1–3 impression mass is broad single-word brand terms (e.g. "hitachi" 424k impr at pos 10.5, "jcb" 507k impr at pos 4.6) where the SERP is dominated by the brand's own properties and intent is ambiguous — those inflate impressions without realistic click potential. **Prioritize CTR work on the commercial "{model} price" terms in positions 4–10, not the broad brand heads.**
