# 11 — 90-Day Execution Roadmap

> Prioritized by **Impact × Effort × Confidence × Business Value**. Business value = proximity to a qualified lead. Sequencing principle: **measure → convert → harvest → scale → compound.** Owners are role placeholders.

## Priority scoring key
- **Impact** (lead/revenue): 1–5 · **Effort**: S/M/L · **Confidence**: H/M/L · **Biz value**: ★1–5
- **TTI** = time to impact.
