# DesiMachines.com — 90-Day SEO & Organic Growth Execution Blueprint

A complete, lead-first organic growth blueprint for **DesiMachines.com**, India's all-brand construction-equipment buying platform. Built to be implemented by an in-house SEO/growth team starting immediately.

**Prepared:** 25 June 2026 · **North Star:** qualified organic **leads** (RFQs, calls, WhatsApp, dealer/OEM/bulk enquiries) and revenue — not traffic.

---

## The one-sentence thesis
DesiMachines has already won the hard part — **DR 41, 2,369 ranking keywords, 1,176 in the top 3, ~39k organic visits/mo of high-intent "price" traffic** — but it is leaking nearly all of that intent because the pages inform buyers instead of capturing them; **the highest-ROI move in the next 90 days is to bolt a WhatsApp + RFQ conversion system onto the pages that already rank**, then harvest position 4–20 quick wins and scale high-intent programmatic pages.

## The 5 things to do first (from `11`)
1. **Instrument GA4 lead events** (WhatsApp/RFQ/call) — you can't manage leads you can't measure.
2. **Ship the WhatsApp + 2-field RFQ system on the top 30 pages** — the biggest lever.
3. **Connect the two Google Sheets + GSC + GA4** — turn estimates into first-party truth.
4. **Harvest the position 4–20 quick wins** (jcb 3dx price #12, jcb on road price #10, crane machine #7, hydra crane #4 …).
5. **Disavow the toxic backlink cluster** — a real, specific risk found in the live data.
