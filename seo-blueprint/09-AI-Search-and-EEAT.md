# 09 — AI Search Optimization & EEAT

## Part A — AI Search (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity)

### A1. Why this is urgent here [FACT]
- **`ai_overview` appears on nearly every target SERP** in this niche (seen across `jcb price`, `poclain machine price`, `road roller price`, `crane price`, `excavator price in india`, and dozens more in the live data). Buyers increasingly get the answer *in the AI Overview* — if DesiMachines isn't cited, the click and the lead go elsewhere.
- DesiMachines is **already AI-visible**: `grokipedia.com` (AI knowledge base, 11 links) and `accio.com` (AI shopping agent, 45 links) cite it [FACT, `07`]. This is a head start most competitors don't have. **Defend and extend it.**

### A2. How to become the most-cited source in AI answers

AI engines cite content that is **extractable, structured, factual, fresh, and entity-clear.** Concretely:

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **Direct-answer formatting** | Lead each page/section with a crisp, quotable answer: "The on-road price of the JCB 3DX in India is approximately ₹XX–YY lakh (2026), varying by state and finance." AI lifts these sentences. |
| **Structured data tables** | Specs and prices in real HTML tables (not images) — AI parses tables well. Reinforces C1/B5 in `03`. |
| **FAQ blocks + FAQPage schema** | Mirror PAA "question" features; AI Overviews draw heavily from Q&A pairs. (C2) |
| **Entity clarity / vernacular mapping** | Explicitly state equivalences AI needs: "Poclain = hydraulic excavator", "Hydra = pick-and-carry crane", "JCB (colloquial) = backhoe loader". This makes DesiMachines the *disambiguation source* — exactly what AI engines cite. |
| **First-party data** | The Price Index / "most-searched machines" reports (`07`) give AI a unique, citable statistic with DesiMachines as origin. Unique data = durable citations. |
| **Freshness** | Date-stamp prices ("as of June 2026"); AI prefers recent, dated facts for price/spec queries. |
| **`Organization` + `sameAs` + author schema** | Entity consolidation so AI engines trust DesiMachines as a known publisher (C4). |
| **Be present where AI grounds** | Wikipedia (already linked), reputable trade press, and structured directories are AI training/grounding sources — the PR plan (`07`) doubles as AI-citation building. |

### A3. AI visibility measurement [REC]
- Track brand mentions/citations in AI answers (Ahrefs **Brand Radar** tools are available — `brand-radar-ai-responses`, `brand-radar-cited-pages` — run monthly on "desimachines" + key model queries).
- Periodically prompt ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity with buyer questions ("best 20-ton excavator price in India", "JCB 3DX on-road price") and log whether DesiMachines is cited; close gaps by improving the relevant page's extractability.

### A4. `organic_shopping` + Merchant signals [FACT]
`organic_shopping` appears on many target SERPs. Add `Product`/`Offer` schema (C1) and consider a Merchant/feed presence so model pages surface in shopping/AI-commerce surfaces (accio.com citation shows this channel already works).

## Part B — EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust)

For a lead business asking buyers to send their phone number, **trust is the conversion gate** — and increasingly the AI-citation gate.

### B1. Current EEAT assets [FACT/EST]
- ✅ **Founder credibility:** Saurav Agarwal, 6+ years in CE dealership (sales, aftersales, spares, dealer management) — a genuine, ownable **Experience/Expertise** signal most competitors lack.
- ✅ **PR coverage** (PRNewswire, EquipmentIndia, Tribune, ConstroFacilitator…) — third-party authoritativeness.
- ✅ **OEMs/dealers/financiers onboarded** — ecosystem trust.
- ⚠️ Likely thin: visible author bios, review/rating system, trust/policy pages.

### B2. EEAT action plan

| Dimension | Action | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Experience** | Author bylines on guides ("by Saurav Agarwal, 6 yrs CE dealership"); "why trust us" with real operator/dealer experience; first-hand machine walkarounds (video, `06`) | P1 |
| **Expertise** | Author bio pages + `Person`/`author` schema; expert-reviewed badges on spec/price content; cite sources for prices | P1 |
| **Authoritativeness** | Press/"as featured in" logo strip; iCEMA/association affiliations; the Price Index as proprietary authority asset; Wikipedia/trade citations | P1 |
| **Trust** | Verified-dealer badges; clear contact (phone/WhatsApp/address) + `Organization` schema; privacy policy, terms, "how we make money" transparency (neutrality is the brand — say it explicitly); user reviews/ratings with `aggregateRating` schema; HTTPS (E4) | **P0** (trust gates leads) |

### B3. Reviews / social proof system [REC]
- Add buyer/operator **reviews & ratings** on model pages (also enables `aggregateRating` rich results — visible in SERPs and AI).
- Display real lead/usage proof ("600+ buyers/month", dealer count, machines listed).
- Collect and show **dealer/OEM testimonials** — these de-risk the enquiry for the next buyer and strengthen the two-sided value prop.

### B4. The neutrality moat (EEAT + positioning)
DesiMachines' unbiased, all-brand stance is simultaneously its **EEAT trust signal**, its **competitive moat vs OEMs** (`05`), and its **AI-citation advantage** (AI prefers neutral, comprehensive sources). **State it explicitly on-site** ("We don't sell machines — we help you choose the right one and connect you to verified sellers") and in schema/about content. This single positioning reinforces conversion, ranking, and AI citation at once.
