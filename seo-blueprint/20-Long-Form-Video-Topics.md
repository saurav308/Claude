## 2. How the five were ranked

Each candidate was scored on five things, in this order of weight:

1. **SEO value** — GSC impressions at stake on pages DesiMachines already ranks, how far those pages sit from the top-3, and whether Google already shows a video thumbnail or carousel for the queries (if Google is not showing video, a video cannot win the slot).
2. **Lead value** — CPC as a proxy for what the click is worth, and whether the topic feeds RFQ, finance or rental leads.
3. **Audience pull** — YouTube competitor density, Hindi phrasing demand, and the channel's own engagement data.
4. **Shorts overlap** — none / partial / covered.
5. **Pairing readiness** — is there a page to embed on today, and does it need the lift.

Volumes are Ahrefs monthly estimates for India; impressions are 90-day GSC totals. CPC is USD.
