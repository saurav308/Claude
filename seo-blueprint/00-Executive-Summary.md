## 1. The thesis in one paragraph

DesiMachines has, in ~16 months, built something most Indian B2B sites never achieve: **genuine organic authority** (DR 41, ~2,369 ranking keywords, **1,176 keywords already in the top 3**, ~39k est. monthly organic visits in India). The category architecture (`/excavator/`, `/backhoe-loader/`, `/crane/`, model pages, `/compare/` pages) is already a textbook programmatic structure. **The problem is not traffic — it is that almost none of this traffic is being engineered to convert into enquiries.** Ahrefs intent flags show the ranking portfolio is overwhelmingly *informational + commercial "price-research"* intent and almost **zero transactional** intent. The buyer is standing in the showroom asking "how much?" — and the site is handing them a spec sheet instead of a salesperson. **The single highest-ROI move in the next 90 days is to bolt a disciplined lead-capture and RFQ system onto the pages that already rank**, then widen the funnel with position 4–20 quick wins and high-intent programmatic pages. Content volume is secondary; conversion architecture is primary.

## 2. The single biggest lever (read this even if you read nothing else)

**DesiMachines ranks #1–#3 for hundreds of `"[brand/model] price"` and `"[model] on road price"` queries — the exact moment of purchase research — but captures these as page views, not leads.**

Evidence (live Ahrefs, India, 25 Jun 2026):
- `jcb price` (90,000 vol) → **#1**, ~6,234 visits/mo to a single blog URL
- `jcb price in india` (23,000) → top-3
- `hydra machine` (13,000), `poclain machine` (10,000), `jcb 3dx price` (9,200), `crane machine` (7,600), `farana crane` (7,400), `road roller` (8,100) — all top-cluster
- The portfolio is flagged `is_commercial: true` / `is_informational: true` and **`is_transactional: false` on virtually every keyword.**

**What this means:** every one of these pages is a high-intent buyer asking a price question. Today they read, then leave to call a dealer. The fix is not more traffic — it is to put a **frictionless "Get best on-road price / Request quote on WhatsApp"** capture on every price and model page, with sticky CTAs, a 2-field RFQ, and instant WhatsApp deep-links. **A realistic 2% → 4–6% lead conversion lift on existing price-page traffic alone is the difference between a few hundred and a few thousand qualified enquiries per quarter — with zero new content.**

This is why CRO (`08`) is sequenced into **Week 1**, not "later."

## 4. The five strategic pillars (90 days)

1. **Convert what already ranks (CRO + lead system).** Highest ROI, fastest payback. Week 1–4. → `08`
2. **Harvest position 4–20 quick wins.** ~Dozens of 4k–90k-volume commercial terms one push from page-1 top slots. Week 1–6. → `04`
3. **Scale high-intent programmatic pages.** Model × city, "on-road price" pages, used/rental/finance, compare pages. Month 1–3. → `04`, `06`
4. **Clean & compound authority.** Disavow toxic links, replace with digital PR + OEM/dealer/association links; win AI-Overview citations. Month 1–3. → `07`, `09`
5. **Build the durable moat: topical authority + first-party data.** Price indices, ownership-cost calculators, buying guides no OEM will publish. Month 2–3+. → `06`
