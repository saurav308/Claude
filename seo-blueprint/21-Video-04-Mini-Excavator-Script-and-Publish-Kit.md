## 0. The video in one breath

A buyer in a small town hears "mini JCB" and assumes two things: it is the cheap machine, and it is the beginner's machine. Both are wrong. On DesiMachines' own listings a 3-ton mini is ₹26–28 lakh and a 2WD JCB 3DX is ₹30–32 lakh, a gap of only ₹4–6 lakh. The mini is not the cheap machine; it is the *small* machine, and the reason to buy a small machine is the work, never the price. The video walks the five things that decide it: what the machine is and which size does which job, the full price list with that shock inside it, where the mini wins (the gate, the trailer, the diesel), where it loses (depth, bucket volume, hire rate), and a one-line rule the buyer can apply at his own gate with a tape measure. It closes on the brand principle: *hum bechte nahi, aapko kharidne mein help karte hain* — which is why the video tells the small machine's weaknesses too.
